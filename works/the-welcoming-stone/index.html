The Welcoming Stone迎客石迎客石The Welcoming Stone

Medium媒介
Woodcut print with chine-collé on handmade paper手工纸上的木刻版画与薄纸裱贴(chine-collé)

A print of a family stone, shaped like a bowing figure, placed at our doorstep as a symbol of hospitality. Reflecting traditional Chinese values of respect and politeness, this work honors its cultural significance and enduring presence.一幅描绘家中一块石头的版画。这块石头形似一个躬身行礼的人,被安放在我家门口,作为好客的象征。作品体现了中国传统中尊重与礼貌的价值观,致敬这块石头的文化意义与它长久的存在。
