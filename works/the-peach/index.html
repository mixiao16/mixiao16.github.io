The Peach桃桃The Peach

Medium媒介
Stone rubbing with mokuhanga frame拓印,水印木刻边框

A rubbing of a peach-shaped stone, framed with mokuhanga. In Chinese culture, the peach symbolizes longevity and carries wishes for luck and happiness. This work honors the stone’s form and cultural significance, blending traditional techniques with symbolic meaning.一块桃形石头的拓印,四周以水印木刻作框。在中国文化中,桃象征长寿,寄寓着对好运与幸福的祝愿。这件作品致敬这块石头的形态与文化意义,把传统技法与象征意涵融为一体。
