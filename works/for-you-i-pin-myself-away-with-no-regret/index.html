This is a heartfelt quote from an ancient Chinese poem that speaks of unconditional love and devotion. This Risograph screen print, mixed media artwork, is presented in a unique 11x17 inch art book shaped like a cocoon, symbolizing both the fragility and warmth of love. The soft, delicate binding, created with fragile thread, mirrors the tender nature of the emotions conveyed in the poem. Every element of the book is designed to embody the essence of unconditional love—gentle, unwavering, and tender, inviting the viewer to connect deeply with the timeless sentiments of devotion and longing.标题出自一首中国古诗中深情的诗句,诉说无条件的爱与奉献。这件结合孔版印刷(Risograph)、丝网印刷与综合材料的作品,以一本形似茧的 11×17 英寸艺术家书呈现,象征爱的脆弱与温暖。以纤细丝线完成的柔软装帧,映照诗中所传达情感的温柔。书中的每个元素都旨在体现无条件之爱的本质——温柔、坚定、细腻,邀请观者深深感受那份关于奉献与思念的永恒情怀。
