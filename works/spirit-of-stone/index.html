Spirit of Stone石魂石魂Spirit of Stone

Medium媒介
Screen print on clear acetate roll透明醋酸胶片卷上的丝网印刷

A screen-printed work on clear acetate, creating a floating spine-like form of the scholar’s stone. Representing the traditional Chinese spirit, the scholar’s stone embodies toughness and strength, standing as a symbol of resilience and cultural heritage.一件印在透明醋酸胶片上的丝网版画,构成一个悬浮的、如脊柱般的文人石形态。文人石代表着中国传统精神,体现坚韧与力量,是韧性与文化传承的象征。
