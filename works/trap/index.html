Trap陷阱陷阱Trap

Medium媒介
Woodcut print, silkscreen on plexiglass木刻版画,亚克力板丝网印刷

“Trap” is a captivating woodcut print that incorporates silkscreen printed plexiglass, creating a dynamic interplay of textures and light. The piece depicts a butterfly ensnared in a web, symbolizing themes of entrapment and vulnerability. The silkscreen print on the plexiglass adds depth and a subtle shading effect, enhancing the emotional intensity of the work. The juxtaposition of traditional woodcut techniques with the modern use of plexiglass invites viewers to reflect on the delicate balance between freedom and restraint, capturing a poignant moment that resonates with both fragility and strength.《陷阱》是一件结合丝网印刷亚克力板的木刻版画,营造出肌理与光线之间生动的交织。画面描绘一只被困在网中的蝴蝶,象征着束缚与脆弱。亚克力板上的丝网印刷增添了纵深与微妙的明暗效果,加强了作品的情感张力。传统木刻技法与亚克力这一现代材料并置,邀请观者思考自由与束缚之间微妙的平衡,定格一个既脆弱又坚强、令人动容的瞬间。
