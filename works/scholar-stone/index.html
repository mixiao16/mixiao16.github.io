Scholar Stone文人石文人石Scholar Stone

A full-shape rubbing of a scholar’s stone, set alone on pale paper. Ink is worked into every ridge, fold and hollow, so the stone’s surface—the marks left by wind, water and time—is carried onto the sheet as a dense field of light and dark.一幅文人石的全形拓,独置于淡色纸面之上。墨被细细拓进每一道棱脊、皱褶与孔洞,石面上风、水与时间留下的痕迹,便化作纸上浓淡交织的肌理。
