Heart of Stone石心石心Heart of Stone

Medium媒介
Stone rubbing with silver leaf拓印、银箔

A stone rubbing highlighting the cracks of a heart-shaped form with silver leaf rather than concealing them. Inspired by the philosophy of scholar’s stones (gongshi), this work explores the beauty of imperfection—embracing flaws as essential to identity and strength. In Chinese tradition, scholar’s stones are treasured not for perfection but for their natural irregularities, which reveal the passage of time and the forces of nature that shaped them. Just as scholars found wisdom and inspiration in the unique contours of these stones, this piece reflects the idea that brokenness is not a weakness but a testament to endurance and transformation. By accentuating rather than disguising the fractures, Heart of Stone embodies the Daoist philosophy of embracing the natural state of things, finding beauty in impermanence, and recognizing that true strength lies in authenticity.一幅心形的拓印,以银箔突显而非掩盖它的裂痕。受文人石(供石)哲学的启发,这件作品探索不完美之美——把瑕疵视为身份与力量不可或缺的一部分。在中国传统中,文人石受珍视并非因为完美,而是因为它们天然的不规则,这些不规则显露出时间的流逝与塑造它们的自然之力。正如文人从这些石头独特的轮廓中获得智慧与灵感,这件作品也表达了这样的理念:破碎不是软弱,而是坚韧与蜕变的证明。通过强调而非掩饰裂痕,《石心》体现了道家顺应事物本然状态的思想——在无常中发现美,并认识到真正的力量在于真实。
