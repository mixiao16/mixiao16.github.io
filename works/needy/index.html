Needy渴求渴求Needy

A multi-colour print of a woman in profile, eyes closed, a single red line running from her eye. Moths settle along her ear, neck and shoulder; her clasped hands are bound in loose thread, set against deep blue and a red circle.一幅多色版画:侧脸的女子闭着双眼,一道红线自眼中垂下。飞蛾停落在她的耳边、颈项与肩头;合握的双手被松散的丝线缠绕,背后是深蓝的底色与一轮红色的圆。
